# 阶段 09 · 最强拒稿论证（对齐 SciForge Phase 14 子步骤）

> 定位：对抗式"攻击—防御"自审。同一 agent 先切到"攻击者"写一段约 200 词的最强拒稿论证，再切到"裁决者"逐点拆解，暴露真正承重的 `still_unresolved` 问题。detect-only，不改研究产物。

## 1. 处理的问题（事实）

**"The non-negotiable goal: force the agent to commit to one damaging line of attack rather than hedge, then honestly triage what the research actually answers."**

平衡弱点表会漏掉最致命的拒稿段落；强制承诺、不淡化。最适合带 ≥5 个定理级环境的理论论文。

## 2. 输入

### 2.1 检索的知识节点
| 节点类 | 关键属性 | 来源 | 是否必须 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Claim | `statement / CLAIMS_FROM_RESULTS.md` | Phase 7 | 必须 |
| DerivationOutput | 推导结果 | Phase 7a | 必须（或 CLAIMS） |
| Contribution | `statement / kind` | Phase 7e | 必须 |
| ReviewPoint | `axis / attack` | 上游 review | 可选 |
| SectionDraft | `section / body` | Phase 8 | 可选 |
| PriorReviews | — | — | **禁止使用** |

### 2.2 检索的 schema 定义
- `application/ReviewPoint.md`——`axis / attack / response / severity`（攻击落点，task-w）。
- `application/Contribution.md`——被攻击的贡献声明。
- `application/Evidence.md`——逐字引文（裁决时核对 file:line 证据）。

### 2.3 输入产物文件
`derivation_output.md` + `verification_report.md`、`derivation.py`（SymPy 证明）、`CLAIMS_FROM_RESULTS.md`、`paper/main.tex` + `sections/*.tex`（可选）。

## 3. Skills 转移函数

| skill | 角色 | 输入 → 输出 |
|---|---|---|
| `support/kill-argument` | Phase 14 子步骤（双角色） | 推导+claims → KILL_ARGUMENT verdict |

### 原始 prompt 原文（逐字）
- "Stress-test the headline claims of a research output against the strongest possible rejection argument, then the same agent switches role to defend point-by-point and surface still-unresolved critical issues."
- "Task: construct the single best argument to reject this research in approximately 200 words; write the worst-case rejection memo a senior area chair would produce after reading the derivation output and claims"
- "Zero-context constraint: do not consult any prior self-reviews, fix lists, or summaries; this must be a fresh, zero-context adversarial pass"
- "Approximately 200 words total (do NOT exceed 250)"
- "Single argument, not a list — pick the most damaging line of attack and develop it"
- "Cite specific file:line locations or equation numbers when accusing"
- "Tone: dispassionate but uncompromising. Do NOT hedge. Do NOT acknowledge mitigations the research might have made elsewhere"
- "Output: just the rejection memo, nothing else."
- 裁决："an independent area-chair adjudicator examining whether the current research text answers a hostile reviewer's rejection memo"；"read the attack point-by-point and rule, from the current source files alone, whether each point stands or falls"
- 三分类："answered_by_current_text / partially_answered / still_unresolved"
- "Attack must commit. Single argument, ~200 words. No 'consider also' hedge. The whole value is in forcing the reviewer to pick the most damaging line."

### 推理逻辑（解释）
零上下文攻击（禁读历史 self-review）→ 约 200 词单一最强攻击（必须引 file:line 或公式号）→ 角色切换为独立 area-chair → 逐点三分类裁决 → 计算 verdict。价值在于"强制选最致命的单一攻击线"，而非罗列弱点。

## 4. 输出

### 4.1 知识节点
- `ReviewPoint`(task-w, severity=fatal)、`KillArgument`(attack/decomposed_points/still_unresolved，建议新增)
- `AttackMemo`（逐字攻击文本）

### 4.2 产物文件
`paper/KILL_ARGUMENT.md`、`.sciforge/verdicts/KILL_ARGUMENT.json`、`.sciforge/traces/kill-argument/<date>_runNN/`（双角色 trace）、`KILL_ARGUMENT.html`。

## 5. 门与有界回退

| 门 | 判据 | 阈值 | 失败去向 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 攻击词数 | ATTACK_LENGTH | ~200，≤250 | 超限重写 |
| 分解点数 | DEFENSE_DECOMPOSITION | 3–7 atomic points | — |
| PASS | `still_unresolved == 0` | 0 | — |
| WARN | any partially_answered at major+ | — | 修复 |
| FAIL/unresolved_critical | ≥1 still_unresolved at critical | — | BA 回 Phase 2 换题 |
| NOT_APPLICABLE | <2 定理环境且无 scope claims | — | — |
| BLOCKED | paper_compile_failed / source_files_missing | — | — |
| PASS 校验 | `validate_verdicts.py` still_unresolved=0 | 0 | FAIL |

**有界回退**：FAIL 且 critical → 若为研究级问题记入 conclusion/limitations；若为写作级问题 → 排队进下一轮 auto-review-loop。KILL loop-back：写完产物后 **Stop**，orchestrator 必须暂停并把 kill case 呈给人类（除非 `human_skip=true` / `kill_checkpoint=false`），确认记入 `APPROVAL_LOG.txt`，然后回 Phase 2 换想法。本 skill 自身无轮次上限（单次对抗 pass）。

## 6. 事实 / 解释 / 不确定
- **事实**：~200/≤250 词、3–7 点、still_unresolved=0 才 PASS、零上下文约束、强制人工检查点。
- **解释**：把 kill-argument 定位为"反自欺"的最后防线——它故意用零上下文攻击避免 agent 用已知缓解措施自我安慰。
- **不确定**：`NOT_APPLICABLE/headline_unstable`（标题或摘要近 2 次 commit 内改过）是原文明确条件，引用时应保留。
